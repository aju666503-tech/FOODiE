Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · This is the current project draft and must be reviewed by the canteen/operator before public launch.

1. Information collected

FOODíE may collect information you enter when using the application, including your name, email address, account password (stored as a password hash), order details, selected fulfillment method, and delivery address when you provide one. The application also records operational events such as account activity and order updates.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, receive and process orders, coordinate preparation with the relevant stall, display order history and status, support inventory and administration, and investigate technical or operational issues.

3. Stall access

Staff accounts are assigned to specific stalls. Staff should only be able to access order items and operational information needed by their assigned stall. Administrators can access platform-wide operational information.

4. Payments

The current application uses a demonstration payment flow only. It does not transfer real funds or connect to a live GCash, Maya, bank, or marketplace payout service. Do not enter payment PINs, banking passwords, or other account credentials into FOODíE.

5. Sharing and service providers

Order information is made available within the application to the customer, relevant assigned stall staff, and authorized administrators for order fulfillment and administration. No live payment processor is currently integrated. If external services are added later, this notice must be updated to identify the relevant providers and data flows before launch.

6. Security and retention

The project includes password hashing, session protections, CSRF checks, and role/stall-based access controls. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. The operator must define and publish a retention schedule, backup process, and contact channel before public deployment.

7. Your choices and requests

Users should be given a way to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. The operator must add verified contact details and a request-handling process before public launch.

8. Changes

When this policy changes materially, users may be asked to review and acknowledge the updated version before continuing to use customer account features.

This draft is intended for the FOODíE project and is not legal advice. Have the responsible institution/operator review it for compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and applicable rules.
