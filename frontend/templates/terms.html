Important

FOODíE is currently a demonstration ordering and canteen-management platform. Some features record operational activity but do not perform real-world financial transfers or guarantee services that the canteen has not formally arranged.

01

Account responsibility

Provide accurate and up-to-date account information. Keep your password private and do not share your account with other people. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account. FOODíE staff will never need your personal e-wallet PIN, bank password, or account recovery code.

02

Menu, prices, and availability

Menus, prices, product descriptions, stock quantities, and stall operating hours are managed by the canteen operator and participating stalls. Availability may change. If an item becomes unavailable or a stall cannot fulfill an order, the affected order portion may be reviewed, cancelled, or adjusted through the application’s order-management process.

03

Orders involving multiple stalls

A single checkout may include items from multiple participating stalls. FOODíE records a combined customer order while each stall receives and manages its own fulfillment portion. Preparation times and progress may differ between stalls. Check the order details and follow the status shown for each stall portion.

An order is subject to acceptance and fulfillment by the relevant stall. Submitting checkout information does not guarantee that every item can be prepared if stock or operating conditions change.

04

Pickup and delivery

Choose the fulfillment method offered at checkout and provide accurate details when requested. Pickup orders are collected at the designated location. Delivery status, including “On the way,” is used only for orders placed for delivery. Delivery availability, service area, fees, and timing depend on arrangements actually provided by the canteen or stall; FOODíE does not independently guarantee a delivery service.

05

Payments and receipts

The current GCash/Maya online payment experience is a demonstration only. It does not connect to live e-wallet accounts, collect real funds, or transfer money to stalls. A demonstration payment reference or receipt is not proof of a real-world payment.

Cash-on-pickup or cash-on-delivery amounts must be paid to the authorized stall representative when the order is collected or delivered, as applicable. A cash payment is recorded as received only after the assigned staff member confirms physical receipt. If live payment processing is introduced later, the operator must publish the applicable payment methods, fees, and refund procedures.

06

Cancellations, adjustments, and refunds

Eligible customers may request cancellation of an individual stall portion or an entire order, subject to the order’s current preparation status and the operator’s rules. A request is not final until it has been reviewed and approved by an authorized administrator. Once approved, the cancelled portion is removed from the amount owed and related inventory adjustments are recorded.

FOODíE may record refund requests and their administrative status. The record itself does not transfer money. Any real refund must be completed separately through the actual payment provider or the operator’s approved cash-refund process. Demo GCash/Maya transactions cannot produce a real-money refund.

07

Acceptable use

Use FOODíE only for legitimate account, ordering, and canteen-management activities. Do not submit false orders, interfere with the service, attempt to access another person’s account, or view information belonging to a stall or user you are not authorized to access.

08

Changes, questions, and support

The responsible canteen or institution may revise these terms when the service, policies, or applicable requirements change. Material updates may require customers to review and acknowledge the new version before continuing to use customer account features.

Before public deployment, the operator must publish verified contact details and a clear channel for questions, complaints, and order concerns. Do not rely on placeholder contact details.

Review before public launch

This document is a project draft, not legal advice. The responsible canteen/institution should review and approve it, confirm its actual operating procedures, and add verified contact information before accepting real orders or payments.
